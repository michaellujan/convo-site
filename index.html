How it works

Pick a real-life scene, like ordering coffee, a doctor's visit, or a job interview, and talk it through out loud with a tutor who plays the other person. Gentle corrections appear under what you said without interrupting the conversation. Afterwards, Convo picks the phrases you found hardest, plays them in a natural voice, and scores your attempts sound by sound with tips on what to change.

Ten minutes a day is enough. Your history and saved phrases stay on your iPhone.

Subscription

Convo Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly through your Apple Account after a free trial. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in the Settings app under your name › Subscriptions.
